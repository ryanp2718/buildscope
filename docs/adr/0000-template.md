## Context

The forces at play: the requirement, the constraint, the failure, or the tradeoff that made a decision
necessary. State the problem so a reader who was not in the room understands why a choice had to be made.

If a measurement forced the decision, cite the dated report in `docs/evidence/` rather than restating the
number. If no measurement exists, say so plainly - a decision taken on judgment alone is legitimate, but
the reader is owed the distinction.

## Decision

The choice, stated plainly. What the system will do from now on.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it forecloses. Include the costs accepted, not just
the benefits.

## Alternatives considered

Each option seriously weighed, and the specific reason it lost. An alternative dismissed in one line was
not seriously weighed; either give it its reason or leave it out.

## Revisit when

The observation that would reopen this decision. Not a date - a trigger. A decision with no conceivable
trigger is either a truism or an unexamined assumption, and both are worth noticing here.

## References

Design docs, evidence reports, code, and data artifacts that ground the decision.
